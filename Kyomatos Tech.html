Questions

Before you start.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the number of pages and features. The planner gives you an estimate in weeks, and we confirm it after reading your brief.

Can I change my mind after sending the brief?

Yes. The brief is a starting point. We agree the final scope with you before any building begins.

Do you handle the domain and hosting?

We can set up hosting on Netlify and connect your domain, or work with what you already have.

Can customers pay online?

Yes. We can connect Nigerian payment providers such as Paystack and Flutterwave.

Will it work well on a phone?

Every page is designed for phones first, because that is where most of your visitors will open it.
